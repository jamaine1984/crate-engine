## Poly Haven Download Pipeline
- API: `https://api.polyhaven.com/assets?t=models` (needs User-Agent header)
- Files: `https://api.polyhaven.com/files/{model_name}` → gltf.1k.gltf.url + includes
- Convert: `npx gltf-pipeline -i input.gltf -o output.glb --binary`
- Models under 20MB kept, over 20MB skipped
