OTR Strategy — Maximizing Per-Mile Revenue

The Backhaul Advantage

The key to OTR profitability is never driving empty. Chicago's central location means there are always loads going out AND loads coming back.

Outbound: Pick high-paying load to destination
Backhaul: Pick return load from destination city
Result: Get paid BOTH directions
Example: Chicago→Atlanta ($2,500) + Atlanta→Chicago ($2,000) = $4,500 in 3 days

OTR Operating Details

⏱️ Hours of Service (Non-CDL 26ft)

A major advantage of a non-CDL box truck (under 26,001 lbs GVWR):

No federal HOS regulations for non-CDL vehicles under 10,001 lbs in most cases
For 26ft trucks (10,001-26,000 lbs): HOS applies IF crossing state lines commercially
ELD required for interstate commerce over 10,001 lbs
14-hour driving window / 11 hours max driving per day
Still far more flexible than CDL-required vehicles

⛽ Fuel Economics (OTR)

26ft diesel box truck: 8-12 MPG
Diesel avg: $3.50-$4.00/gal (Midwest)
Cost per mile: ~$0.35-$0.50
Rate per mile: $2.00-$5.00
Net margin per loaded mile: $1.50-$4.50
